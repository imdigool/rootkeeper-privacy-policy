▎ Rootkeeper ("the bot") is operated by [YOUR NAME / ALIAS]. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL OR SERVER].
▎
▎ What the bot stores. Each community that installs the bot gets its own database, hosted by Root. It holds: the community's bot settings; moderation cases, warnings and notes (user IDs, moderator IDs, reasons, time); member role backups; automod and antinuke settings and snapshots of channels, roles and permissions; onboarding and verification state; ban appeals; and support tickets. For tickets, while a ticket is open the bot records the messages in the ticket channel (author, text, attachment names and links) so it can produce a transcript.
▎
▎ Short-term memory. To support edit and delete logs, the bot keeps the most recent messages (up to 5,000 for up to 24 hours) in memory only. They are not written to disk.
▎
▎ What our server stores. The bot talks to a small service (hosted on Cloudflare) that stores: the community ID and bot version; the IDs of recently active members; ban-appeal records (appeal code, user ID, the appeal text and the decision); an audit log of actions by the bot owner; and short-lived technical records such as request identifiers and rate-limit counters. It does not store message history.
▎
▎ What we do not do. We do not sell or share your data, show ads, or use it to train AI models. We do not read messages outside of the features above.
▎
▎ Who else handles data. Root (the platform) and Cloudflare (hosting) process data as our service providers. The bot downloads a public list of phishing domains; no user data is sent for that.
